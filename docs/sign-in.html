Using AIngram · Step 2

Sign in to your admin page

The admin page is where you look after your AI's memory: read it, approve the rules, let AIs in and forget things. It lives on your own AIngram server.

Before you start

  • Your wallet, set up in Chrome, Brave or Edge on a computer.
  • Your server's web address, from whoever set it up. It looks like https://memory.example.com.
  • Your wallet's address must be the one the server was set up with. Only that wallet can sign in.

Sign in

  1. Open your admin page

    Go to your server's address with /admin on the end, like https://memory.example.com/admin. Bookmark it.

  2. Click your wallet's button

    The page lists the wallets it finds in your browser. Click yours. If it says no wallet was found, unlock your wallet and reload the page.

  3. Let the page see your address

    The first time, your wallet asks whether to connect to this site. This only shares your address, which is safe to share.

  4. Sign the sign-in message

    Your wallet shows a message starting with your server's address and "wants you to sign in with your Ethereum account". Check the address is yours, then sign. No fee, nothing sent.

✓
You're in

You'll see your wallet's short address at the top right, and the admin page's tabs.

A quick tour

TabWhat it's for
OverviewThe latest notes, a button to check the chain, downloads, backups, and your server's connection address.
MemoryEvery note, by subject or by search. Open one to read it in full, or forget it.
KeysThe AIs you've let in. Add one for Claude Code, or switch one off.
PolicyThe rules. Read them, change them, approve them.
ImportBring in notes your AI already has from somewhere else.
ActivityEvery change you've approved, with the exact message you signed.

Looking is free; changes ask again

Being signed in lets you look around. Each change pops up in your wallet again, saying exactly what will happen, and only happens if you sign. So if someone ever got hold of your signed-in browser tab, they still couldn't change anything without your wallet.

You're signed out after 30 minutes without activity, 8 hours after signing in, or when you click Sign out. If you ever signed in on a computer that isn't yours, use Sign out everywhere on the Overview.

!
The first time, you'll see "No policy is approved yet"

That's expected. Your AI's memory stays switched off until you approve the rules. That's the next step.
